Akash DTH Middleware Platform

2023 – 2025 | Softshore Technology

The Akash DTH Middleware Platform acts as the central integration layer between Akash’s core Direct-to-Home (DTH) system, the MyAkash mobile application, and multiple external financial service providers. The middleware was designed and developed using a microservices architecture to handle high transaction volumes while ensuring extensibility, fault tolerance, and long-term maintainability. This architecture allows the platform to scale seamlessly and remain resilient under heavy operational load.

The platform enables secure, real-time financial transactions by integrating leading Mobile Financial Services and Payment Service Providers such as Rocket, bKash, Nagad, and other PSPs into a unified transaction flow. Strong emphasis was placed on reliability, security, and operational stability, along with simplifying the onboarding process for new financial partners without disrupting existing services. Throughout the project, I worked closely with business and technical stakeholders to align system behavior with operational requirements and ensure smooth production readiness.

Role: System Architect, Developer

Technologies: Spring Boot, PostgreSQL
